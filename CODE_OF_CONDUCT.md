# Code of Conduct

## Decentralized Governance

Open3diy is a small repository, maintained individually, but its goal is to follow a decentralized web3 model. This means that, although administration and maintenance currently fall to a single person, open participation and collective decision-making are encouraged as the community grows.

Proposals, suggestions, and contributions from any member are welcome and considered transparently and collaboratively. The goal is to evolve towards governance where key decisions are made in a decentralized manner, prioritizing transparency, inclusion, and consensus.

The community commits to:

- Promoting equitable participation in decision-making.
- Fostering transparency in governance and development processes.
- Valuing and considering all contributions, regardless of the participant's experience or role.
- Adapting governance structures as the community grows to reflect the principles of decentralization and collaboration.

This approach seeks to ensure that the project evolves openly, resiliently, and in alignment with web3 values and community collaboration.

Additionally, by pursuing a decentralized and incentivized model, a space is ensured where collective interest moves in the same direction. In this type of model, participants often contribute resources or capital, which fosters genuine commitment and discourages disruptive or malicious behavior. This is possible thanks to the use of tokens and the implementation of a DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization), where participants have a voice and vote proportional to their involvement and contributions. Thus, decisions and actions within the community tend to align with common goals, promoting collaboration, mutual respect, and the achievement of shared objectives, while reducing the incidence of trolls or malicious actors, since their participation implies a real commitment to the project.

## Expected Behavior

To maintain a healthy and collaborative environment, all community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate of other participants.
- Listen actively and value different points of view.
- Accept constructive criticism with openness.
- Avoid offensive, discriminatory, or violent language.
- Respect the privacy and personal data of others.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Derogatory comments related to gender, identity, sexual orientation, disability, race, religion, nationality, or others.
- Threats, violence, or incitement to violence.
- Publishing private information of others without their consent.
- Any other conduct that may be considered disruptive or harmful to the community.

## Enforcement of the Code of Conduct

Violations of this code can be reported through the repository's official channels or directly to the main maintainer. All reports will be treated seriously and confidentially.

Consequences for non-compliance may include warnings, temporary or permanent expulsion from the community, and other actions deemed appropriate depending on the severity of the case.

## Attribution and Respect for Licenses

As an open source project, it is essential to respect attributions, references, and licenses of any content, code, or resource used or shared in the community. All members are expected to:

- Give proper credit to the original authors of ideas, code snippets, documentation, or reused resources.
- Comply with the licenses associated with any material, whether copyleft, copyright, or others, ensuring that their use and distribution are carried out according to the established terms.
- Avoid plagiarism and misappropriation of others' work.
- Promote transparency regarding the origin of contributions and resources.

Respect for attributions and licenses strengthens trust, collaboration, and the integrity of the project, aligning with the fundamental principles of free and open source software.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, both online (repository, issues, pull requests, forums, chats) and at in-person events related to the community.

## Acknowledgments

This code of conduct is inspired by initiatives from open source communities such as [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/) and seeks to adapt to the values of decentralization and collaboration of web3.
