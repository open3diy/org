We do our best to reference open, freely accessible, and explanatory sources. However, we cannot guarantee that every linked site remains open, free, or free of fraudulent content at the time you visit it. Websites change, and what was appropriate when a link was added may not reflect the current state of that site.

**We are not responsible for:**

- The accuracy, legality, or content of any external site.
- Sites that have become paywalled, fraudulent, or misleading after the link was published.
- Any harm, loss, or inconvenience arising from visiting a linked external resource.

By following any external link, you do so at your own discretion and risk.

## How to Report a Problematic Link

If you find a link that points to a fraudulent, paywalled, or otherwise inappropriate site, we encourage you to report it. As an open repository, anyone can [open an issue](https://github.com/open3diy/org/issues) describing the problem. The community will review and correct it as part of our continuous improvement process.

## Our Commitment

Whenever possible, links in Open3DIY point to:

- Open and freely accessible documentation or resources.
- Official project pages or well-known community references.
- Sources that are explanatory and educational in nature.

If you see room for improvement, do not hesitate to contribute. This is what Open3DIY is about.
